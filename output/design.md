# Campus Customs design direction

- **Palette:** Deep Yale navy `#00356B`, ink `#071C2F`, warm ivory `#F5F0E7`, parchment `#E8DFD0`, and brass `#B58A45`. The darker, quieter palette gives the collection a clubroom feel and makes product photography and calls to action feel considered.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond handles editorial headlines; DM Sans handles navigation, prices, and utility text. The contrast creates hierarchy and makes the shop feel more like a curated collection than a template catalogue.
- **Motion and presentation:** Product cards lift and reveal a brass edge on hover; the hero has a slow ambient light drift; detail images have a restrained zoom; the chat panel opens with a soft rise. These cues reward exploration without slowing shopping.
- **Conversion details:** Brass price accents, clear size pills, restrained labels, and a stronger “Ask Campus Customs” panel make the next action easier to see while preserving the traditional collegiate tone.
